PAKA Poker 16 3D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how PAKA Poker 16 3D ("PAKA Poker", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: player display name and, if you register an account, your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Game information: player identifier, game activity, multiplayer events, rankings, progression, achievements, and virtual-item entitlements.
  • Purchase information: Google Play product identifiers and purchase tokens used to verify subscriptions. Google processes your payment details; we do not receive your full card details.
  • Optional M-PESA information: phone number, payment amount, transaction identifiers, and transaction status when you request a deposit.
  • Technical information needed to operate online multiplayer, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate multiplayer games, maintain player progress, provide support, verify purchases, process optional payment requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve reliability.

Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate the app, including Google Play for subscriptions, Safaricom M-PESA for optional payment requests, and infrastructure providers that host the PAKA Poker service. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online system is completely secure.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may play as a guest, avoid optional purchases, and decline optional M-PESA deposits. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, email masimbajeremiah@gmail.com. We may retain limited records where required by law or for fraud prevention.

Children

PAKA Poker is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

International processing and changes

Information may be processed where our service providers operate. We may update this policy as the app changes and will post the revised effective date on this page.
