# PAKA Poker IP protection readiness

This is a preparation record, not a filed application or legal opinion.

## Recommended protection routes in Kenya

1. Register the source code, audiovisual assets, text, music and other original game content through the Kenya Copyright Board (KECOBO).
2. Conduct a KIPI trademark search for `PAKA POKER`, the logo and relevant service classes before filing a trademark application.
3. Preserve trade secrets for unpublished server-side anti-cheat, matchmaking, security and operational methods through access controls and confidentiality agreements.
4. Seek a Kenyan patent agent's novelty assessment only if an inventor can identify a new technical solution beyond game rules, business methods, visual presentation or ordinary software implementation.

## Patent intake needed before drafting claims

- Legal name, nationality and address of every inventor
- Applicant/owner legal name and address
- Written assignment where the applicant is not the inventor
- Exact technical problem and the new technical mechanism that solves it
- Earliest conception and implementation dates
- Every public disclosure, repository publication, demo, sale or discussion and its date
- Closest known competing systems or prior art
- Architecture diagrams and reproducible implementation details
- Countries where protection may be required

The live GitHub deployment and public repository may be relevant public disclosures. Do not make additional disclosures about a potentially patentable technical mechanism until a qualified patent professional has assessed novelty and filing strategy.

## Filing boundary

Only the named inventor or an authorized representative should approve inventorship, ownership, declarations, legal terms, filing scope and payment. A provisional Kenyan application must be followed by a complete application within the applicable deadline stated by KIPI.
